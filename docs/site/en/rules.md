# Rules

Tayan is played with 2 to 13 players. Everyone is dealt a few cards, and nobody sees anyone else's cards. The players take turns bidding poker hands that they think exist **in the pooled cards of all players**, not only in their own hand.

## A round

1. Every active player is dealt as many cards as their counter says (2 or 1 at the start, chosen automatically from the number of players, or by the host).
2. The starting player names a hand. They cannot check, as there is nothing to check yet.
3. Each next player must either **raise** (name a hand strictly higher than the last one) or **check** (challenge the last declaration). There is no passing. If the last declaration is the highest possible hand, the only option is to check.
4. After a check everyone shows their cards. If the declared hand exists in the pool, **the player who checked** gets an extra card; if it does not, **the player who declared** gets it.
5. A player who reaches the elimination limit (6 cards by default, so holding 5 is still fine) is out. The last player left wins.

The next round is started by the next active player at the table, regardless of who lost. The seating order is drawn once at the start of the game.

## The deck

The deck always ends at the ace. In the default automatic mode the lowest rank depends on the number of players and the settings, so that strong hands (four of a kind, straight flush) do not show up too often. The host can also choose the full deck (from 2) or a custom lowest rank. The deck does not change during the game.

## Declarations

Every declaration names a concrete hand with all its parameters, for example "full house: queens full of nines", not just "full house". See the [hand ranking](/en/hands).

A hand that is physically impossible with the cards in play may still be declared; it simply loses when checked.

## Disconnections

By default the game waits for a disconnected player without a limit. The host can set a time limit, after which the player is out as if they had lost. Independently, after 2 minutes of inactivity on someone's turn the other players can vote to remove them.
